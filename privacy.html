Interactive examples

The graph, guided conversation and calculator use fictional or user-selected values in your browser. They do not connect to business systems or send those inputs to a Sonaway server. This website does not set analytics or advertising cookies.

Project brief form

The form prepares an email draft and displays a copyable brief. It does not submit information to a server. If you choose to send that email, Sonaway receives the information you include and uses it to discuss and respond to your enquiry.

Hosting and external services

The hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP addresses, requested pages and browser details to deliver and secure the website. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts, which receives the technical information needed to serve those requests. Links to other sites are subject to those sites’ own privacy practices.
