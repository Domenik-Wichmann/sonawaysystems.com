Website privacy

This website shows illustrative demos. They run in your browser and do not save your work to a server. The contact form prepares an email in your own email app. The Ask Sonaway chat searches our public business information. When AI search is enabled, your question may be sent to Cohere for retrieval and to OpenRouter and its selected model provider with relevant public passages to draft an answer. Please use this form for general business questions and leave out personal or confidential details. We rank approved questions by aggregate use across the chat and FAQ cards. Other questions count toward broad topics. These popularity totals contain no visitor messages or identities. We use a short-lived cookie and hashed identifiers to limit requests. Answers may be temporarily cached for up to 24 hours. Google Fonts is requested when the page loads. Email us at domenik@sonaway.com with privacy questions.
