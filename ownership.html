NO FORCED LOCK-IN

A system you own, with a documented handoff.

You receive the agreed system and information in an environment you control. Our maintenance service supports that asset; it does not make your operational knowledge conditional on a Sonaway subscription.

01

Your environment

We deploy into the agreed accounts and infrastructure, with access you control and third-party services billed as agreed.

02

Your operational information

Your source material, structured knowledge and agreed operational data remain under your control, with documented access and export arrangements.

03

Code & documentation

Receive agreed project-specific repositories, deployment procedures, configuration, documentation and data-export guidance. Pre-existing reusable components and third-party dependencies come with explicit licensing terms.

04

Support without mandatory lock-in

Keep Sonaway responsible for the agreed upkeep, or appoint your own team or provider. Maintenance scope and responsibilities are documented separately from ownership.

HOW AN ENGAGEMENT WORKS

How we scope, build and maintain your system.

We start by understanding the work, reviewing your sources and agreeing the useful tools to build. Scope, deliverables, acceptance criteria, timelines and responsibilities are agreed before significant development begins.

We plan maintenance alongside the build: what gets checked, who approves changes and how faults are handled. New modules and major development remain separately scoped.

01

Discovery & scoping

Gather documents and staff know-how; identify the workflows, gaps and constraints with your managers.

02

Build & integration

Build the agreed interfaces and integrations in iterations your team can review.

03

Validation & training

Check source handling, permissions, approval steps and practical staff use against the agreed criteria.

04

Handoff & optional care

Provide the agreed code, access and documentation, with defined ongoing care or a complete handoff.

AFTER LAUNCH

Ongoing care from Sonaway.
Control stays with you.

Your staff contribute practical knowledge and tell us what changed. We organise it, check it against sources, prepare revisions and maintain the agreed tools. Your designated managers approve operational changes before publication.

OPTION A

Documented handoff

If you prefer to run it independently, we provide the agreed code, data, access and operating instructions. Your team or chosen provider assumes maintenance and future changes.

OPTION B

Sonaway-managed care

We maintain the agreed content, training, source checks, reporting and technical connections. You have a specialist to work with as information changes and problems arise, without assigning staff to run a knowledge-management platform.

Infrastructure and third-party services may carry their own costs. Reusable libraries and third-party components may be licensed rather than assigned outright; rights are set out transparently in the contract.
WHAT WE LOOK AFTER

Ongoing care for the knowledge and the system.

Buying a tool creates another thing to look after. Our ongoing service covers the agreed information checks, training updates, reporting and technical maintenance, with review frequency and response times defined in advance.

01 / INFORMATION QUALITY

Content and data quality assurance

We combine automated validation, comparison with source material and human review to flag incomplete or conflicting information. We investigate discrepancies and prepare changes for your designated approvers; operational changes remain subject to manager approval.

02 / INSIGHT & IMPROVEMENT

Analytics built around your questions

We bring recurring questions, learning activity and available process data into reports built for your operation. We review useful patterns with you and help turn findings into clearer procedures, focused training or better customer information.

03 / TECHNICAL CARE

Maintenance with someone accountable

We maintain agreed source processing, APIs and integrations, investigate failures and correct problems within the support scope. Your staff can contribute information from the working day without taking on the technical administration.

WORKING TOGETHER

Clear responsibilities.
Agreed support.

Your team’s part

Share source material and operational changes, and nominate managers to confirm facts and approve publication. Your team decides policy and safety requirements; we show staff how to contribute and use the tools.

Sonaway’s part

Organise and cross-check information, investigate discrepancies, prepare revisions, maintain the agreed tools and explain the reporting. We handle the specialist work while your managers retain operational authority.

Service scope, review frequency and response times are agreed in advance. New integrations, major features and additional reporting projects are scoped separately. Analysis of workflow bottlenecks requires suitable process data.
