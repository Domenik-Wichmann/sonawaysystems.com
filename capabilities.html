Knowledge bases, assistants and API integrations.

These are implementation components we scope, build and test with your team. We agree the sources, workflows, access and acceptance criteria before development.

01

Process capture and documentation.

We extract practical know-how from interviews, recordings, photos and existing files. We document procedures and handoffs, checking missing or conflicting steps with your team. Reliable event logs can also support process mining.

INPUTSVoice · photos · documents
OUTPUTSProcess maps · structured SOPs
02

Knowledge base design and governance.

We build a searchable knowledge base with source records, versions, local variations and named owners. Automated checks and human review flag gaps and contradictions for your designated approvers.

SOURCE RECORDArrival procedure · v3
CONTROLOwner · approval · history
03

Staff and customer knowledge assistants.

We build phone-friendly chat and voice interfaces that retrieve approved guidance by role, property and date. Answers include source references, clarification and escalation when the available information is insufficient.

RESPONSEApproved answer + source

Custom APIs and system integrations.

We connect agreed APIs, files and databases, building mappings, authentication, scheduled syncs or webhooks. We include validation, logging, error handling and the approval gates needed for external actions.

CONTROLSAccess · validation · logs

Training, reporting and access controls.

We build these modules against your actual procedures and responsibilities. Ongoing care keeps agreed learning, reports and controls aligned as the operation changes.

Ongoing maintenance for the knowledge and the tools.

01

Knowledge and source maintenance

We check submitted information against sources, investigate contradictions and prepare updates for your approvers. We maintain the related procedures and training within the agreed scope.

02

Usage and process-data reviews

We review agreed question, training and process-data reports with you, identify matters to investigate and help improve the relevant guidance or workflows.

03

Technical maintenance and support

We look after agreed integrations, source processing and technical upkeep, investigate failures and correct problems. Your staff use the tools and contribute operational knowledge.

Project scope and ownership.

Start with one useful module or a connected implementation. We agree the requirements, build and test the system, and deliver the agreed code, data and documentation. Choose ongoing specialist care or a complete handoff.
