ABOUT SONAWAY

Practical knowledge.
Built into the way you work.

Sonaway is a founder-led service that builds and maintains knowledge bases, assistants, training tools, dashboards and integrations for small and midsize businesses. We work with your team to turn practical know-how into tools people can use during the working day.

OUR PERSPECTIVE

We start with how your business actually works.

The procedure may be in a binder, the latest change in a message and the useful detail in an experienced colleague’s head. Staff are serving customers and running facilities; maintaining a knowledge system is another job competing for their time.

We gather documents and staff knowledge, check the gaps with your managers and build the agreed tools around approved information. That can include phone-based answers, reusable training, update workflows, dashboards and connections to existing systems.

With ongoing care, we keep working with your business: checking sources, maintaining training and integrations, investigating problems and reviewing useful patterns. You own the agreed system and can choose a documented handoff instead.

HOW WE WORK

Who does the work, and how we work with you.

01

Founder-led delivery

Work directly with Domenik Wichmann, responsible for understanding your operation, building the agreed system and organising its ongoing care.

02

Operational reality first

We review the real procedures, people and constraints before proposing tools. We can start with one recurring problem and expand when there is a useful reason.

03

Clear responsibility after launch

Your managers approve operational changes. We maintain the agreed information and technology, with defined scope, review schedules and response times—or a documented handoff to your provider.

WHY CONNECTED KNOWLEDGE

A maintained foundation that supports more uses.

An approved procedure can support a staff answer, an onboarding lesson and a customer notice. We build those uses from shared knowledge, then help improve them as questions and reviews reveal what is missing. The service develops with your operation rather than stopping at launch.

WORK WITH SONAWAY

Tell us what your team keeps having to work around.

Repeated explanations, conflicting instructions or systems nobody has time to maintain: describe the situation. We will discuss the build and ongoing work that could help.
