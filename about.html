A DIRECT WORKING RELATIONSHIP

Practical operations.
Connected systems.

I’m Domenik Wichmann. My background spans catalog data operations, content management, teaching and running a physical product business. The recurring challenge is familiar: valuable knowledge exists, but keeping it accurate, connected and usable takes real work.

Sonaway Systems brings that problem together with custom software, knowledge engineering and AI-assisted workflows. I work directly with businesses to understand the operation, build a useful system and define how it will be maintained.

The focus is on operational knowledge: how people find answers, communicate changes, learn their work and coordinate across teams. The technology follows the needs of the business.
