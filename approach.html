Let’s talk
FROM OPERATIONAL PROBLEM TO WORKING SYSTEM

Built around your business.
Clear from the beginning.

A useful outcome, a defined scope and a system your people can actually use. We agree the delivery, ownership and ongoing responsibilities before the work begins.

01 / DISCOVERY

Find the right problem to solve.

We discuss your operation, review representative information and map the people and processes involved. Together we define the first useful outcome, the boundaries and how we’ll evaluate it.

A scoped implementation proposal, responsibilities and success criteria.

02 / KNOWLEDGE FOUNDATION

Establish what the system should know.

We ingest the agreed sources and capture missing context with your people. Related knowledge is connected, contradictions are raised, and source evidence is retained. Access rules, local variations and approval responsibilities are designed explicitly.

A reviewed knowledge structure and an agreed model of context and access.

03 / IMPLEMENTATION

Build around the way people work.

We create the agreed assistant, update workflow, interfaces, dashboards and integrations. The first implementation can be narrow: one team, one process or a specific class of questions.

A usable system with the agreed features and integrations.

04 / VERIFICATION

Test questions—and changes.

We work through representative tasks with your users: finding an answer, changing a policy, handling an exception, applying permissions and reviewing a proposed update. We check the evidence and the behavior, not just the interface.

Acceptance checks, operating instructions and a rollout plan.

05 / CONTINUITY

Choose the support that fits.

You can take over the delivered system with the agreed documentation, retain Sonaway for ongoing management, or combine your internal responsibilities with ours. Additional development is scoped separately.

An agreed handoff or a clearly bounded managed service.

OWNERSHIP & CONTINUITY

A system that can stay
with your business.

We can deliver into accounts and infrastructure you control, with the code, data access and documentation agreed for your project. Continuing to work with Sonaway is a service decision—not an unclear exit process.

Manage it with Sonaway

We take responsibility for the agreed knowledge operations and maintenance, with your designated people approving business decisions.

  • Defined intake volumes and review cadence
  • Agreed response windows and escalation
  • Content and answer-quality review
  • Maintenance and service reporting within scope

Take it forward yourself

Receive a planned handoff to your team or another provider. We establish the practical requirements before development.

  • Agreed custom source code and data exports
  • Account, infrastructure and deployment access
  • Operating procedures and maintenance guidance
  • Dependency, licensing and running-cost documentation

Custom deliverable rights, reusable components and third-party licenses are specified in the project agreement. Hosting, AI services and other third-party costs may continue after handoff.

COMMERCIAL STRUCTURE

We quote based on the sources, operational complexity, integrations, users and level of ongoing work—not just the number of documents you have.
