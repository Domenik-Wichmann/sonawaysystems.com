A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Let’s make
the practical
details clear.

Is this a platform subscription?

Sonaway Systems provides custom consulting, implementation and optional management. We scope the work around your operation. Implementation, ongoing service and infrastructure costs are agreed separately.

Do we need documents before we start?

No. Existing material helps, but knowledge can also be captured through interviews, voice notes, recordings and process walkthroughs. Your subject experts confirm how the operation should work.

Will our staff have to maintain a knowledge base?

With a managed service, we can handle the agreed intake, organization, quality review and publishing work. Your team supplies changes and approves operational decisions. We can also build guided intake so updates are easier to provide.

Does every change automatically update everything?

Known, controlled dependencies can support coordinated updates after approval. Independent documents, local exceptions and inferred knock-on effects need review. We define what is connected, what can be refreshed automatically and what requires a person’s decision.

Can we start with one team or location?

Yes. A defined process, department or site is often a useful starting scope. Shared knowledge can then support additional teams and sites as separate, planned expansions.

Can we keep the system if we stop using your service?

We can structure delivery for a complete handoff, including agreed code, data exports, account access and operating documentation. Reusable components and third-party software have their own agreed rights and costs. The handoff is defined before development.

What does your business keep having to explain?

Start with one process, one team, or one recurring problem. We’ll work out what a useful system would look like.
