Common questions

Why does my business need a knowledge base?

When a packer on the night shift asks in Spanish which label goes on an order, they can get the answer in Spanish and open the approved procedure in Spanish, too. The same source serves someone asking in English, German, Chinese or another language your business needs. Your team can find answers 24/7, even when the person who wrote the guide is off shift.

Why hire Sonaway if we already use Trainual, Confluence or ChatGPT?

Keep the tools that work for your team. We capture how the job is done, reconcile conflicting instructions and publish the procedures you approve. Then we build only what’s missing, whether that’s a searchable database you control, staff permissions or a connection to the assistant you already use.

A connection may let an assistant retrieve documents. The quality of its answers also depends on how it searches and which sources it can use. We check what your tools expose and can set up meaning-based search over approved content. See how we connect your tools →

Do you use AI to write our procedures?

We use AI to help transcribe recordings and organize source material. A human reviews the evidence, writes the procedure and takes gaps back to your staff. The instructions come from your business, and someone you designate approves them before publication.

Which businesses benefit most?

Often it’s a growing business where the founders or original crew still field every question. Packing facilities, fabrication shops, hotels and franchise groups are good examples. We capture the work while it happens so people can spend their shift on the floor.

What can you build for multiple locations?

Your front desk can look up a late-arrival procedure while guests see just the arrival instructions on your website. We can also connect the guest guide to a voice agent that answers calls. You decide which information is public, which stays with staff and when a question goes to a person. Explore custom systems →

How can you help us train staff?

We start with the tasks your managers want people to learn and turn approved procedures into role-specific onboarding. Once the system is in use, recurring staff questions can show where a guide or lesson needs work. We can deliver a short refresher before the next shift that needs it.
