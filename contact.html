What happens after you reach out?

We’ll talk through the work and agree on a written scope and quote before starting. You can run a pilot first and decide what to do next after reviewing it.

01

Start with one role.

An experienced employee and any checklists you already have are enough to begin. Allow about a week for setup after we agree on the scope, subject to availability and what you want to test.

02

Capture the handoffs.

If another role owns a decision or handoff, we’ll bring that person in to document what affects the pilot role. If you want the second role documented in full, we’ll agree on the additional scope and cost before taking it on.

03

Keep what you’ve paid for.

You keep the approved documents and any agreed findings. A pilot can include a basic document Q&A trial. If you want to keep a custom system running, we’ll estimate hosting, software and usage costs and discuss who will maintain it.
